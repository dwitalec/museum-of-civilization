The Strategic Map

Our Core Strategic Advantage Decades of proprietary driver data and Snapshot telematics give us the industry's sharpest risk segmentation — we price micro-segments competitors have to price as averages. That edge is amplified by a dual distribution engine (direct-to-consumer at scale plus a deep independent-agent network) and a culture that holds every book to underwriting profit, not premium growth.

Every acquisition candidate must be weighed against the eight ways M&A destroys value — before it destroys ours.

The 8 M&A Failure Archetypes

01

Liability Underwriting

Underestimating hidden claims reserves, IBNR gaps, or regulatory exposure baked into the target's back book.

02

Perpetuity Error

Assuming today's loss ratios, retention, and combined ratio trends continue forever, uncorrected by cycle.

03

Peak Extrapolation

Valuing the target off a cyclical high — soft pricing, low cat losses — that will not persist.

04

Structure Unchanged

Assuming distribution, agent relationships, and state filings hold static post-close.

05

Policy Bet

Betting the entire thesis on one product line, state, or regulatory ruling instead of a resilient book.

06

Substitution Blindness

Missing that InsurTechs, telematics-first entrants, or embedded insurance can substitute the offering entirely.

07

Capability Illusion

Believing the target's underwriting and claims talent, data, and models transfer cleanly into our systems.

08

Closing Risk

State DOI approval delays, rate-filing freezes, or integration slippage erode value before the deal ever closes.
